    Impact Evaluation

    Manual Meeting Notes vs. CogniSync AI

    See the tangible operational contrast between unstructured ad-hoc scribbles and verified AI-distilled action intelligence.

    Manual Note-Taking (Status Quo)

    Fragmented & Subjective

    • Long & unstructured: Wall-of-text notes where crucial points are buried under conversational tangent banter.
    • Inconsistent formatting: Note quality depends heavily on who was typing; varying levels of detail and acronym confusion.
    • Decisions hard to locate: Requires 15+ minutes of manual scanning to confirm whether a proposal was actually approved.
    • Missed deliverables: Action items lacking explicit tags or dates inevitably slip past sprint review deadlines.
    CogniSync AI Intelligence

    Objective & Instantly Actionable

    • Concise executive synthesis: A 3-sentence high-level brief delivering 78% compression without loss of intent.
    • Standardized structure: Clean key points, verified decisions, and structured accountability tables every single time.
    • Clear consensus tags: Decisions isolated with distinct checkmarks and architectural rationale preserved.
    • Trackable ownership & dates: Explicitly assigns tasks to owners with priority badges, checkable status, and date filters.
    Human-Centered Methodology

    How We Designed This

    A 5-phase Design Thinking exploration addressing collaborative fatigue, cognitive overload, and commitment drift in modern cross-functional teams.

    MC

    Maya Chen

    Lead Product Manager & Capstone Team Lead

    Runs 18-24 hours of sprint, design, and stakeholder meetings weekly across remote time zones.

    Core User Goals

    • Keep cross-functional engineering, UX, and marketing teams aligned after marathon sprint reviews.
    • Eliminate 45 minutes of manual post-meeting transcription every afternoon.
    • Ensure zero dropped commitments or ambiguous task ownership.

    Key Frustrations

    • "I cannot engage in strategic debates when forced to frantically type meeting minutes simultaneously."
    • Teammates disagree on what was decided three days later because verbal consensus wasn't logged.
    • Meeting recordings sit unwatched in cloud drives because nobody has 90 minutes to review.
    INSIGHT 01 Users want clear synthesis over verbatim transcripts; bullet points must be actionable.
    INSIGHT 02 Assignee initials and due dates are 3x more impactful than generic summaries.
    INSIGHT 03 Zero-latency offline availability builds immediate trust without privacy friction.
    “Cross-functional project leaders and distributed teams struggle to maintain execution velocity because long, unstructured meetings bury critical decisions and verbal action items in conversational clutter, causing missed deadlines and cognitive fatigue.”

    The Design Challenge

    How can we extract the high-signal "signal" (decisions, tasks, dates) from low-signal conversational dialogue without imposing burdensome manual tagging workflows on participants during the call?

    Success Metrics Defined

    1. Over 75% reduction in meeting reading time.
    2. 100% precision in capturing named owners and explicit dates.
    3. Sub-second browser generation with zero cloud dependencies.

    How Might We (HMW) Explorations

    HMW 01: Instant Decision Isolation How might we algorithmically isolate sentences representing firm consensus from speculative proposals without complex neural training?
    HMW 02: Accountable Attribution How might we automatically map commitment verbs ("I will", "please deliver") to explicit team members and deadlines in one pass?
    HMW 03: Seamless Handoff How might we export structured meeting outcomes directly into team productivity ecosystems (Markdown, tables, checklists)?

    Chosen Solution Architecture

    A hybrid local NLP pipeline coupling speaker diarization parsing, term-frequency heuristic scoring, regex-guided decision extraction, and date pattern recognition into an intuitive two-panel boardroom dashboard with exportable Markdown & checklist interactions.

    What This Working Prototype Demonstrates

    This prototype proves the end-to-end viability of client-side meeting intelligence without external server latency or privacy vulnerabilities.

    1. Multi-Modal Transcript Ingestion

    Supports raw text pasting, drag-and-drop .txt file ingestion, and real-time audio capture via browser Web Speech API with graceful fallback.

    2. Local NLP Extraction Engine

    Runs client-side TF scoring, decision cue matching, and owner/deadline resolution with an architecturally marked summarizeWithLLM() hook.

    3. Interactive Action Item Matrix

    Provides checkable task states, dynamic owner filter pills (Priya, Marcus, Elena, David), priority badges, and visual initials.

    4. Boardroom SaaS Design System

    Engineered with dark obsidian hues, glowing blobs, glass cards, micro-waveform animations, and WCAG-compliant contrast.
